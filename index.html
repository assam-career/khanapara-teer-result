Khanapara Teer result time

The F/R result normally appears between 4:00 and 4:15 PM, and the S/R result follows at about 5:00 PM. These are rough times. On some days the shooting finishes early or late, so the number may show up a little off schedule. Play usually runs Monday to Saturday.

About Khanapara Teer common numbers

You will often hear about "common numbers", "house" and "ending" numbers. These are simply guesses that people make by studying old results. Looking through the archive can be interesting, and you may notice that some numbers repeat and others stay away for months. But that is a pattern in the past, not a promise about tomorrow.

Teer numbers come from arrows shot at a target, so no chart, dream or formula can tell you the next result. Please play responsibly, never spend money you cannot afford to lose, and ignore anyone selling "sure" numbers. You must be 18 or older.
